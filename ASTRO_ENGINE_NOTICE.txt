AI ஜோதிடர் v0.9 — calculation engine notes

Astronomy Engine 2.1.19 is bundled into the Android web assets at build time.
License: MIT. Copyright (c) 2019-2025 Don Cross.
Source: https://github.com/cosinekitty/astronomy

Jyotish convention in this build:
- Tropical geocentric apparent ecliptic positions -> Lahiri/Chitrapaksha sidereal conversion
- Mean Rahu / Ketu
- Whole-sign houses
- Lagna from GAST + longitude and true obliquity
- Nakshatra / pada from Moon longitude
- Vimshottari Mahadasha display from Moon nakshatra

The calculation layer is deterministic and runs inside the app; it does not require a remote astrology API.
